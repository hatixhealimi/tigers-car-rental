Driving Around Macedonia

The best places to visit by car.

Explore Skopje

Start your journey in Skopje, the capital of Macedonia. Explore the Old Bazaar, visit the Stone Bridge and enjoy the city's restaurants, cafés and nightlife.

Visit Lake Ohrid

Lake Ohrid is one of the most beautiful destinations in the region. A road trip to Ohrid gives you the freedom to explore the lake, the old town and nearby viewpoints at your own pace.

Discover Mavrovo

If you prefer mountains and nature, drive towards Mavrovo National Park. The area offers beautiful mountain roads, forests, hiking trails and peaceful places to stop along the way.

Enjoy The Journey

Renting a car allows you to create your own route and discover places that are difficult to reach by public transport. Take your time, drive safely and enjoy everything Macedonia has to offer.
